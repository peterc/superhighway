As 2020 draws to a close, I wanted to look back over the past 12 months and highlight what happened in the React ecosystem this year. As such, here's a brief month-by-month breakdown of the year that was.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🗓 React in 2020 --- Month by Month

## January

-   Dan Abramov [reflected on his previous decade of work](https://overreacted.io/my-decade-in-review/).
-   The TypeScript team [moved to Gatsby](https://www.gatsbyjs.com/blog/2020-01-23-why-typescript-chose-gatsby/) for its docs site.
-   [Styled Components v5](https://medium.com/styled-components/announcing-styled-components-v5-beast-mode-389747abd987) was released.

## February

-   React Native grew up and got its own domain name: [reactnative.dev](https://reactnative.dev).
-   An official [Redux template for Create React app](https://github.com/reduxjs/cra-template-redux/releases/tag/v1.0.0) hit 1.0.
-   [React Query 1.0](https://github.com/tannerlinsley/react-query) appeared.

## March

-   [RedwoodJS](https://redwoodjs.com) started to pop up on our radar. We expect to cover this a lot more in 2021.
-   Coronavirus began to throw in-person React events into disarray.

## April

-   [Preact 10.4.0](https://github.com/preactjs/preact/releases/tag/10.4.0) was released.
-   ZEIT [rebranded as Vercel](https://vercel.com/blog/zeit-is-now-vercel). They've since continued to grow and raise more money.

## May

-   [Next.js 9.4 was released](https://nextjs.org/blog/next-9-4) (fast refresh and incremental static regeneration were the headline features).
-   [Recoil](https://recoiljs.org) began to be unveiled.
-   The Gatsby folks [raised lots of money](https://www.forbes.com/sites/davidjeans/2020/05/27/gatsby-website-building-startup-backed-by-index-ventures-raises-28-million/?sh=6f35d3597f3e).

## June

-   The React core team [joined a Facebook employee walkout](https://twitter.com/dan_abramov/status/1267544361929256966) in solidarity with the Black community.
-   MDN began to [publish React tutorials](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn/Tools_and_testing/Client-side_JavaScript_frameworks/React_getting_started).

## July

-   Adobe [unveiled React Spectrum](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/blog/introducing-react-spectrum.html), a collection of libraries to build UIs.
-   Microsoft [announced React Power BI](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/en-us/blog/announcing-react-power-bi/), for everyone who loves business reporting.
-   [Next.js 9.5](https://nextjs.org/blog/next-9-5) came out.

## August

-   Facebook revealed more about [how they use React themselves](https://engineering.fb.com/2020/07/30/web/facebook-com-accessibility/).
-   [React 17.0 RC1 came out](https://reactjs.org/blog/2020/08/10/react-v17-rc.html) --- and had no new features.

## September

-   Kent C Dodds wrote [a love letter to React](https://epicreact.dev/why-i-love-react/).
-   We learnt more about [React's new JSX transform](https://reactjs.org/blog/2020/09/22/introducing-the-new-jsx-transform.html).

## October

-   [webpack 5](https://webpack.js.org/blog/2020-10-10-webpack-5-release/) was released.
-   [React 17.0](https://reactjs.org/blog/2020/10/20/react-v17.html) final was released.
-   [Create React App 4.0](https://github.com/facebook/create-react-app/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md#400-2020-10-23) was also released.
-   \...as was [Next.js 10](https://nextjs.org/blog/next-10).

## November

-   A new [Redux fundamentals tutorial](https://redux.js.org/tutorials/fundamentals/part-1-overview) was published.
-   The BBC began to [show off its React-based work](https://github.com/bbc/simorgh).
-   [Vue 3.0 came out](https://github.com/vuejs/vue-next/releases/tag/v3.0.0), keeping React on its toes.

## December

-   The [React Christmas advent calendar](https://react.christmas/2020) began again.
-   [React Query 3.0](https://twitter.com/tannerlinsley/status/1338498989918998532?s=21) was released.
